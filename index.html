PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), text, CSV, HTML, JSON, RTF, and other files can be stored. Text comparison varies by format. Files stay on this device.

Duplicate review

Exact duplicates use SHA-256 checksums. Other suggestions use normalised filenames and, when extractable, document text. Nothing is removed automatically.

Recycle bin

Removed plans stay here until you permanently delete them. Original files outside this app are never changed.

Backups

Export a complete ZIP of your library, including original files, categories, notes and recycle bin. Restore on another device by importing that ZIP.

Restoring adds missing records and skips records with the same internal ID. Backups are not encrypted: store them privately.

Important limitations

Browsers cannot silently delete your original files or scan your entire device without permission. Removing a plan affects only the app's stored copy. For older .doc and .xls files, import and exact-byte matching work, but content extraction may not. Scanned/image-only PDFs may need OCR, which this offline version does not include.

Browser storage can be cleared by device cleanup or browser settings. Export regular backups, especially before reinstalling the app.
